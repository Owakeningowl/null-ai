General Facts

  • The AI has full knowledge of your team’s stats, moves, items, and abilities from the start of the fight.
  • Non-attacking moves default to +6 Score (tied with HDM). Exceptions: Nature Power, Memento.
  • Most guaranteed-secondary-effect moves with accuracy above 70 also default to +6 and follow their corresponding status move’s AI — unless already functioning as the HDM.
  • Future Sight also starts with a default +6 Score, but only if no Future Sight/Doom Desire is already queued on either side — otherwise -20.
  • AI usually sees 3 hits for multi-hit moves. Loaded Dice raises this to 4 hits seen; Skill Link to 5.
  • AI calculates both Pokémon’s Speed stats to predict Analytic/Payback. Does not apply to Avalanche.
  • AI does NOT understand the Player’s priority moves — it only reasons using dynamic Speed stats.
  • Phazing moves: Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw.
  • Pokémon that haven’t transformed yet (Wishiwashi, Minior, Castform) are seen by the AI in base form. Castform’s Weather Ball is seen at 1.5x to simulate future STAB.
  • Every scoring function runs in a fixed order (AI_CheckBadMove → AI_TryToFaint → AI_CheckViability → AI_SetupFirstTurn → AI_Risky → AI_PreferStrongestMove → AI_PreferBatonPass → AI_DoubleBattle → AI_HPAware), gated by which flags a trainer/wild mon has enabled. Three additional flag slots (Negate Unaware, Will Suicide, Help Partner) are DEFINED as named constants but have no function attached in this build — reserved/unimplemented. AI_CheckBadMove’s final result is floored at 0 — it can never make a move go negative on its own, only remove its appeal.

Critical pipeline mechanic: once any move’s running score hits 0 or below at ANY point in this chain, the AI stops running further scoring functions on that move entirely for the rest of the turn — a move zeroed out by AI_CheckBadMove never gets a chance to recover via AI_Risky or AI_PreferStrongestMove later, even if those would otherwise like it. All 4 move slots always start at exactly 100 (not 0) when a real choice is being computed. Moves with 0 PP, or disabled/taunted/otherwise illegal, are force-zeroed before any scoring function runs at all. In Doubles, a move that literally cannot target a given battler (CanTargetBattler check) is skipped for that target entirely, not merely scored low.

Difficulty/flag assignment also has a config-variable hook: wild Pokémon can have additional AI flags injected via a build-time variable (B_VAR_WILD_AI_FLAGS) on top of the level-based thresholds below.

Support Pokémon Definition

A Pokémon is classified as Support if:

  • It has at most one damaging move.
  • It has no moves with base power > 75.
  • Pokémon with Imposter are excluded.
  • Moves that don’t count toward the damaging-move total: Fake Out, Feint, Upper Hand, Endeavor, Super Fang, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw, Knock Off, Foul Play, Future Sight, Bug Bite, Pollen Puff, speed-control damage moves (Rock Tomb, Glaciate), and Surf/Dive (Cramorant only).
  • Feeds into Post-KO switch scoring (+2, 10% chance) and its own Mid-Turn Switch trigger — the doc’s own words: “The support class plays a notable role in some Switch decisions.”

Worked example, to make the definition concrete: a Pokémon carrying Knock Off, Fake Out, Toxic, and Protect still classifies as Support — neither Knock Off nor Fake Out count toward the damaging-move limit, and Toxic/Protect deal no direct damage at all, so its real damaging-move count is 0. The same Pokémon with a 90-base-power move added instead of Protect would NOT qualify — that single move breaks the “no move over 75 power” rule outright, regardless of how few damaging moves it has overall.

Why this classification likely exists (reasoned from the pattern below, not stated outright in the source): a Support-classified Pokémon is, by definition, one that isn’t built to threaten real damage — its value is in what it sets up (hazards, screens, status, utility) rather than what it hits for. Both places this label actually changes AI behavior point the same direction: it becomes MORE likely to switch out once it’s in Singles and has been in for more than a turn (the Mid-Turn “Support AI” trigger), and it gets a small bonus to being sent back in specifically when it ISN’T about to just get OHKO’d (the Post-KO +2 bonus, gated behind not already being at -1). Read together, that reads as the AI treating Support Pokémon as more disposable/rotational than attackers or walls — worth bringing in when the coast is relatively clear to do its job, and worth pulling back out again once that job is done or the field gets dangerous, rather than parking it in like a wall would be. This paragraph is inference from the two mechanics, not a claim the source document makes explicitly.

Common Scores To Be Aware Of

  • Highest damaging move (HDM): +6 (75%), +8 (25%)
  • Slow kill (AI kills but is slower than target): +9 (75%), +11 (25%)
  • Fast kill (AI kills and is faster* than target): +12 (75%), +14 (25%)

*Speed ties count as faster; priority kills always count as fast. These totals are literally the +6/+3 kill bonus stacked on the +6/+8 HDM bonus, since a killing move is almost always the HDM too.

Damaging Moves

Never rolled/considered HDM: Explosion, Self-Destruct, Misty Explosion, Final Gambit, Meteor Beam, Future Sight, OHKO moves, Feint, Upper Hand.

If a damaging move kills:

  • AI faster, or priority while AI slower: +6 Score
  • AI slower: +3 Score
  • No kill bonus for: Explosion, Self-Destruct, Misty Explosion, Final Gambit, Rollout, Ice Ball, OHKO moves
  • Kill bonus despite never being HDM: Meteor Beam, Future Sight
  • AI has Moxie, Beast Boost, Chilling Neigh, Grim Neigh, As One (Ice or Shadow Rider), and is faster with a confirmed kill: +1 Score

Damaging priority moves (Feint excluded):

  • AI slower, Player can faint AI, not Mimikyu-Disguise-protected:
    • Priority move is also HDM: +5 Score
    • Else: +11 Score
  • Else if AI slower and holds Eject Button, same conditions: +11 Score

Moves that thaw the user: AI frozen/frostbitten: +12 Score

Choice AI: holding a Choice item blocks all status moves (-20 Score) except Memento, Parting Shot, Baton Pass, Teleport, Chilly Reception, Sleep Talk, Me First, Copycat, Mimic, Transform, Sketch, Nature Power, Assist, Metronome.

Priority Moves - Weakness Policy: priority move can activate it: +12 Score (25%), +8 Score (75%)

Recursive-evaluation moves: Me First, Copycat, Mirror Move, Nature Power, and Mimic don’t have their own scoring — they look up the move they’d actually copy/call and run that move’s own full AI_CheckBadMove/AI_CheckViability logic recursively. Banned-move lists prevent copying certain moves (checked via per-move flags, not detailed further here).

Ability, Field & Weather Immunity Layer

These block or heavily discourage a move regardless of what it is, before move-specific logic runs.

Hard blocks (move can’t even be selected, returns 0 outright):

  • Target has Wonder Guard and the move isn’t super effective
  • Sound move while Throat Chop is active on the user
  • Heal-blocked move while under Heal Block
  • Target is “commanding” (Dondozo/Tatsugiri mechanic)
  • EFFECT_PLACEHOLDER moves (unfinished/reserved effects)

Target ability blocks, -20 Score (full penalty when the move’s actual target has the ability):

  • Good As Gold: blocks any status move outright
  • Dazzling / Queenly Majesty: blocks the user’s priority moves against the field
  • Aroma Veil: blocks mind-affecting moves
  • Sweet Veil: blocks Sleep/Yawn
  • Flower Veil: blocks status and stat-lowering on Grass-types
  • Magic Bounce: blocks Magic-Coat-affected moves
  • Contrary: blocks stat-lowering moves aimed at it
  • Clear Body / White Smoke / Full Metal Body: blocks stat-lowering and Parting Shot
  • Hyper Cutter: blocks Attack-lowering (except Play Nice, Noble Roar, Tearful Look, Venom Drench)
  • Keen Eye / Mind’s Eye: blocks Accuracy-lowering
  • Big Pecks: blocks Defense-lowering
  • Defiant / Competitive: blocks stat-lowering (unless targeting own partner)
  • Comatose: blocks non-volatile status
  • Shields Down (in shield form): blocks non-volatile status
  • Leaf Guard (in Sun, no Utility Umbrella): blocks non-volatile status

Partner ability blocks in Doubles, at HALF penalty (-10 Score) since it’s a secondary consideration:

  • Lightning Rod (Electric redirect), Storm Drain (Water redirect), Magic Bounce, Sweet Veil (also blocks Dark Void), Flower Veil, Aroma Veil, Dazzling/Queenly Majesty

Gen 7+ Prankster vs. Dark-type: status move blocked, -10 Score

Terrain blocks, -20 Score: Electric Terrain blocks Sleep/Yawn on grounded targets; Misty Terrain blocks non-volatile status and confusion; Psychic Terrain blocks the ATTACKER’S OWN priority moves.

Primal Weather blocks, -20 Score: can’t set any of the four basic weathers under a Primal weather; non-Hydro-Steam Water moves blocked under Primal Sun; Fire moves blocked under Primal Rain.

Magic Guard interactions: Poison/Burn/Toxic/Leech Seed against a Magic Guard target: -5 Score. Curse specifically, if the user is Ghost-type and the target has Magic Guard: -5 Score (no chip damage will land).

  • Powder moves against a target immune to powder: -10 Score.
  • Semi-invulnerable target (Fly/Dig etc.) and the AI is faster: -20 Score (target will dodge anyway).

SETUP MOVES

Every stat-raising move shares this base pattern (unless noted otherwise): can’t-rise check (-20), “will I die before benefiting” check (-20, exempted by Sash/Sturdy/Mimikyu Disguise/Substitute), and “will this just get erased” check (-20 if target has Unaware, Haze, Freezy Frost, Topsy Turvy, or Clear Smog — often also if target has Roar/a phazing hit-switch move and the AI still has usable party mons).

Offensive Setup (viability layer)

Tidy Up, Dragon Dance, Shift Gear, Attack Up (and variants), Growth, Work Up (Attack/SpAtk line)

  • Player incapacitated: +3 Score (90%)
  • Else if Player kills AI in 4+ hits: +1 Score (Player faster) / +2 Score (Player slower)
  • Boosted stat already +2 or higher: -1 Score (80%)
  • Tidy Up specifically: if own side has hazards up and party isn’t empty, +2 Score flat instead (prioritizes the hazard-clearing side-effect)

Defensive Setup (viability layer)

Defense Up (and variants), Special Defense Up, Cosmic Power, Cotton Guard, etc.

  • 80% of the time: Player incapacitated +2 Score (90%); Defense boost with Player having only physical moves +1 Score (mirrored for Special Defense); stat already +2+ without Body Press +1 Score: -1 Score

Mixed Setup

No Retreat, Victory Dance, Coil, Bulk Up, Curse, Contrary Superpower/Make It Rain/Overheat, Calm Mind, Quiver Dance

  • Classified Offensive or Defensive based on the PLAYER’s actual moveset split, mirrored for special vs. physical.
  • Curse used by a Ghost-type doesn’t follow setup rules at all — stays default +6 if target isn’t already cursed; HP≤50% blocks it (-20 in CheckBadMove).
  • Contrary Superpower/Overheat/Make It Rain: routes through ShouldSetupOthers/ShouldSetupOffensive specifically when Contrary flips the move into a buff instead of a debuff.

Speed Setup

Autotomize, Agility, Rock Polish, Trailblaze, Flame Charge, Aqua Step, Esper Wing, Scale Shot

  • AI already faster: -20 Score (CheckBadMove)
  • AI slower: +1 Score (80%)
  • Also blocked if target has Roar/phazing and AI has usable party mons (fears being phased before benefiting)

Rapid Spin

Treated as Speed Setup, plus:

  • Best-damage-move / Wrapped / Leech-Seeded: bypasses the speed-setup gate entirely (still scores on damage)
  • No hazards on AI’s own side: standard speed-setup logic applies
  • Viability: AI slower, +7 Score (80%); Leech-Seeded or Wrapped: +1 Score; own-side hazards present: +2 Score (50%) (shared code path with Defog)

Evasion Setup

Double Team, Minimize

  • Blocked by Unaware/Haze-family or phazing-fear the same as Speed Setup
  • AI HP > 90%: +1 Score (80%); else if AI HP > 60%: +1 Score (60%)

Geomancy

  • Requires literally holding Power Herb, or -20
  • Immediately-usable: +9 Score (80%) in CheckViability, +3 Score (25%) as a separate bonus in the general stat-setup block
  • Standard setup death/negation checks apply, plus phazing-fear check

Shell Smash, Belly Drum, Fillet Away, Clangorous Soul

  • Shell Smash specifically: Contrary flips it to raise Def/SpDef instead of Atk/SpAtk; stat stages ≤5 still required even under Contrary
  • Belly Drum/Fillet Away: blocked by Contrary entirely, HP≤50%, or Attack already >+6
  • Viability: target incapacitated +3 Score (90%); can’t-be-KO’d-after-setup +2 Score; would-be-KO’d-after -2 Score (accounts for berries)

Acupressure

  • Blocked if Substitute blocks it or stats already maxed
  • Standard death-check and phazing-fear checks
  • Viability: target incapacitated +3 Score (90%); target needs 4+ hits to kill AI: +1 Score, plus +1 more if AI is faster

Charge

  • Blocked if already charged up, or if target has Roar/phazing (bypasses the SpDef-can’t-rise check entirely in that case)
  • Otherwise needs Sp. Def room like a normal setup move
  • Viability: target has an Electric move: +1 Score (50%)

Defense Curl

Additional to Defensive Setup: has Rollout/Ice Ball and hasn’t used Defense Curl yet: +1 Score (both in the bad-move layer and again as +1 in viability)

Stockpile

  • Blocked at 3+ stacks
  • Contrary blocks it entirely in viability
  • Has Swallow or Spit Up: +1 Score

Spit Up

  • Blocked below 2 stacks (needs ≥2 to be worth using in CheckBadMove’s ≤1 gate)
  • Viability: stacks ≥2: +1 Score

Swallow

  • Blocked at 0 stacks; blocked at max HP; discouraged (-5) at ≥80% HP as a last resort
  • Heals 25/50/100% depending on 1/2/3 stacks, scored through ShouldRecover: +2 Score if worthwhile

Order Up (Commander/Tatsugiri mechanic)

  • Curly commanding: routed through Offensive Setup (Attack) scoring
  • Droopy commanding: routed through Defensive Setup (Defense) scoring
  • Stretchy commanding: its own inline block — AI slower: +6 Score, plus +1 Score (80%); AI faster: -1 Score

Rototiller

  • Requires the user (or, in Doubles, its grounded Grass-type partner with room to grow Atk/SpAtk) to actually benefit, or -20

Gear Up

  • Only useful with Plus/Minus ability; -10/-8 penalties (self) or -20/-10 (Doubles partner-based) if the relevant stat/move-split combo won’t actually benefit

Magnetic Flux

  • Same Plus/Minus mechanic as Gear Up, but for Defense/Sp. Defense; self penalties -20 each stat, partner-based -10/-8

STATUS-INFLICTING MOVES

Sleep (Sing, Dark Void, Yawn)

  • Fully blocked (-20) via AI_CanPutToSleep if the target can’t be put to sleep at all
  • Yawn additionally blocked if the target is already yawned
  • 25% chance, if AI doesn’t see a kill: target can sleep +1 Score; target has Dream Eater/Nightmare/Snore/Sleep Talk +1 Score; target or partner has Hex/Infernal Parade +1 Score
  • Dark Void specifically, Doubles only: +2 Score (~80%), stacks with (falls through into) the standard Sleep scoring above

Poison (Poison Gas, Toxic, Toxic Thread’s fallback)

  • Blocked via AI_CanPoison
  • ~37.5% chance, if no kill seen and target HP >20%: no damaging moves +1; AI has Protect +1; AI has Venoshock/Hex/Infernal Parade/Venom Drench/Merciless +1
  • Toxic Thread specifically: if lowering Speed isn’t worthwhile, takes a -1 first (still might poison); if AI is faster, +1 Score bonus (50%) on top of standard poison scoring

Paralysis (Nuzzle, Thunder Wave, Glare)

  • Blocked via AI_CanParalyze
  • If no kill seen: AI slower-before/faster-after paralysis +2; else Hex/Infernal Parade/flinch moves +2; else Player confused/infatuated +2; else flat +1
  • On-hit variant (Nuzzle-style): Zap Cannon explicitly excluded from the on-hit bonus; Covert Cloak/Shield Dust block it; +6 Score if it can land, PLUS the standalone paralyze scoring above, MINUS a further 50% chance of -1

Burn, Frostbite (Will-O-Wisp, Freezy Frost, Sizzly Slide)

  • Blocked via AI_CanBurn / AI_CanGiveFrostbite
  • ~37.5% chance (96/256), if no kill seen: +1 Score; Player has physical moves (special for Frostbite) +1; else AI/partner has Hex/Infernal Parade +1
  • On-hit Burn variant: Burning Jealousy explicitly excluded; Covert Cloak/Shield Dust block it; +6 Score if lands, falls through into standard burn scoring

Confusion (Confuse Ray, Supersonic, Swagger, Flatter)

  • Blocked via AI_CanConfuse
  • 25% chance, if no kill seen: target can be confused +1; Player paralyzed/infatuated, or AI has Serene Grace+flinch moves +1
  • Swagger additionally: user has Foul Play/Psych Up/Spectral Thief, or holds Mirror Herb: +1 Score
  • Flatter additionally: same list minus Foul Play: +1 Score
  • Doubles, targeting partner: +9 Score (25%), +8 Score (75%) if partner can’t be confused and benefits from the stat swing

Attract

  • Blocked via AI_CanBeInfatuated
  • Singles only: if target would faint from secondary damage before Attract could ever trigger (and target’s faster), skip entirely
  • +1 Score if target already has status, confusion, or is trapped (attract “piles on”)

Yawn

  • Blocked if already yawned, or via the standard sleep-immunity check

STAT-LOWERING MOVES

Every stat-down effect is individually blocked at -20 if the target’s stat is already at minimum, AND blocked by a specific per-stat ability list:

  • Attack-down / Tickle / Venom Drench / Noble Roar: Hyper Cutter, Clear Body, White Smoke, Full Metal Body, Defiant, Competitive, Contrary
  • Defense/Speed/Sp.Atk/Sp.Def-down: Clear Body, White Smoke, Full Metal Body, Defiant, Competitive, Contrary (no Hyper Cutter — that’s Attack-specific)
  • Accuracy-down: adds Keen Eye, Mind’s Eye to the above list
  • Evasion-down: same Clear Body family list
  • Venom Drench additionally requires the target to actually be poisoned already, or -20

On-hit stat-down variants (100% chance only; Covert Cloak/Shield Dust block them entirely):

  • ShouldLower[Stat] worthwhile: +6 Score; else +5 Score
  • Doubles spread target: +1 Score additional

Standalone stat-down moves:

  • Not worthwhile per ShouldLower[Stat]: -2 Score
  • Target’s stat already below default: -2 Score
  • Else: +1 Score (~20%)
  • Accuracy-down specifically: an extra +2/+1/-1 tier based on the AI’s own HP (>90%/>60%/<60%)
  • Special Defense-down specifically uses a slightly different gate (target already lowered, 80% chance -2, else 20% chance +1)

Tickle

Checks BOTH Attack-lowering and Defense-lowering worthwhileness independently, stacking -2 penalties if either isn’t worth it, +1 Score (20%) if it clears both checks.

Captivate

Requires opposite, known genders (not genderless, not matching): -10 Score if it wouldn’t work.

RECOVERY & DRAIN

Recovery Moves (Recover, Slack Off, Heal Order, Roost, Strength Sap, and the weather/HP-scaled ones)

  • At max HP: -20 Score (hard block)
  • HP ≥85%: -6 Score (still usable as a last resort)
  • Else: ShouldRecover(…) true → +1 Score; false → -1 Score
  • Morning Sun/Synthesis/Moonlight: check the boosted 67% Sun-benchmark FIRST (+1 if worthwhile), falling through to the standard 50% check otherwise
  • Shore Up: same pattern but for Sandstorm’s 67% benchmark
  • Rest: requires AI_CanSleep or blocked entirely; +2 Score (not +1) if the AI has a real way to wake up early (Cure Sleep/Status item, Sleep Talk/Snore in kit, Shed Skin, Early Bird, or Hydration+Rain); otherwise +1 Score
  • Pain Split: averages both HP totals: if the resulting HP clears a 30%-of-max benchmark above current AND ShouldRecover confirms it’s worth it: +2 Score; else -1 Score

Swallow — see Setup Moves section (Stockpile family) for its exact healPercent-tiered logic.

Purify

  • Requires target to have a status condition, or -20 (unless target is your own Doubles partner — always heal your ally)
  • Blocked at max HP, discouraged (-6) at ≥90% HP
  • Viability: +1 Score if the user itself has a status to cure

Refresh

  • Blocked (-20) if AI could be KO’d, or if there’s no actual status to cure
  • Viability: +1 Score if a status is present

Drain Moves (Giga Drain, Draining Kiss, etc.)

If target lacks Liquid Ooze:

  • AI at half HP or under, kills target in ≤4 hits: +6 Score
  • AI holds Big Root: +1 Score

ENTRY HAZARDS & FIELD

Stealth Rock, Stone Axe, Spikes, Ceaseless Edge, Toxic Spikes, Sticky Web (generic hazard pattern)

  • Target has Magic Bounce: -30 Score (hard veto — worse than a normal block)
  • Already at max layers (Spikes 3, Toxic Spikes 2) or partner is about to set the final layer: -20 Score
  • Target has 0 usable party mons: -10 Score (viability layer)
  • AI’s first turn, team >1 alive mon: +2 Score (~98%, scaled slightly by exact roll)
  • Existing 1+ layers (Spikes/Toxic Spikes only): -1 Score (~98%)
  • General scaling bonus, EXACT formula: +1 Score at a chance of 192 × (alive mons ÷ total mons) out of 256 — e.g., 3 alive of 5 total ≈ 45%
  • Sticky Web specifically: +3 Score flat on turn 1 instead of the scaling formula (no max-layer concept, single-use effect)

Tailwind

  • Blocked (-20) if already up on your side, or if Trick Room is currently active
  • Player has Trick Room or Taunt: -1 Score (50%) — partial discouragement, not a hard block
  • AI/partner slower than any Player mon on the field: +3 Score
  • Independently, if Player’s own Tailwind is already up and their Tailwind-setter is slower than the target: +3 Score (can stack with the above, up to +6 total)

Trick Room

  • Blocked if partner is already setting it, or if it’s already active
  • Routes through a speed-priority helper; if that check fails: -1 Score, done
  • If target has Trick Room or Taunt themselves: 50% chance to skip the bonus entirely
  • Otherwise: +4 Score

Wonder Room

  • Blocked if already active or partner-duplicated
  • +1 Score if Player’s attacking split doesn’t match their better defensive stat (i.e., Wonder Room would help them attack you); +2 Score (25%) flat on top

Magic Room

  • Blocked if already active or partner-duplicated
  • +3 Score (25%)

Mud Sport, Water Sport

  • Blocked if already active (field or personal status) or partner-duplicated
  • +3 Score (25%)

Gravity

  • Blocked if already active
  • 50% chance of any bonus at all; within that, always +1 Score, with a further 25% chance of +4 Score more (so outcomes are effectively: 50% nothing, ~37.5% +1, ~12.5% +5)

Weather Moves (Sandstorm/Sun/Rain/Hail/Snow)

  • Blocked if that weather (or a Primal version) is already active, or partner is already setting a different weather this turn
  • Hail/Snow specifically: -2 Score if the OTHER snow-family weather is active (prevents Hail↔︎Snow ping-ponging)
  • ShouldSet[Weather] true: +2 Score, plus +1 Score (80%) more if holding the matching extending rock (Smooth/Icy/Damp/Heat Rock)

Terrain Moves (Electric/Grassy/Psychic/Misty)

  • Blocked if partner is setting a different terrain, or this terrain’s already active
  • +2 Score flat, plus +1 Score (80%) if holding Terrain Extender

Imprison

  • Blocked if already imprisoning
  • ShouldImprison true: +3 Score

Ion Deluge

  • Blocked if already active or partner-duplicated
  • If AI has Volt Absorb/Motor Drive/Lightning Rod and predicts the target’s move is Normal-type: +1 Score; always followed by a baseline -1 Score (net 0 or -1)

Fairy Lock

  • Blocked if already active or partner-duplicated
  • Target not already trapped and ShouldTrap confirms: +1 Score, followed by baseline -1 Score (net 0 or -1)

Magnet Rise

  • Extensive exclusion list: Gravity active, existing Magnet Rise timer, Iron Ball held, already rooted/magnet-risen/smacked-down, or simply not grounded to begin with
  • Viability: only relevant if grounded AND target has a Ground move; AI faster: +2 Score (90%, denies the move entirely); AI slower: -1 Score (too late to matter)

Telekinesis

  • Extensive exclusion list: target already telekinesis’d/rooted/smacked-down, Gravity active, Iron Ball, banned species, partner-duplicated

Camouflage

  • Blocked via CanCamouflage helper; no additional viability bonus beyond default

Lucky Chant

  • Blocked if already active or partner-duplicated; no additional viability bonus

Pledge (combo move)

  • Doubles only: if the partner is also about to use a different Pledge move while asleep/frozen, blocked (-20, don’t set up a combo that’ll just fail)
  • Viability: partner also knows a Pledge move: +3 Score

SWITCHING & SACRIFICE MOVES

U-Turn, Volt Switch, Flip Turn, Parting Shot (Hit-Escape family)

  • Blocked (0 usable party mons aside) — Palafin gets a flat +12 Score that overrides everything else
  • Kills Player in more than 3 hits: +6 Score (Parting Shot already defaults to +6 as a status move, so this doesn’t stack additionally for it)
  • Target has Roar/phazing: -1 Score (discourages switching into a guaranteed phaze-back)

Baton Pass

  • Blocked if no stat is raised AND target has Roar/phazing, or if 0 usable party mons
  • Viability: 25% chance of a flat -1; then +1 Score if Substitute/Rooted/Aqua-Ring/Magnet-Rise/Power-Trick is active, or any stat is raised at all

Teleport, Chilly Reception

  • Blocked if 0 usable party mons, or (faster AI about to be KO’d anyway, Sash/Sturdy/Disguise/Substitute exempted)
  • Viability: ONLY scores positively in genuine trainer battles on the Player’s side; kills Player in >3 hits: +1 Score (50%)

Shed Tail

  • Blocked: 0 usable party mons, already Substituted, target is Infiltrator, AI HP ≤50%, or target has a sound move
  • Viability: if faster and can already KO the target: +1 Score (50%); else +1 Score (25%) as a flat fallback

Explosion-family, Memento

  • Blocked entirely: 0-effectiveness matchup, Damp ability anywhere on the field, target’s stats already fully lowered (Memento only), or target holds Clear Amulet (Memento only)
  • If it’s the AI’s last mon and Player still has mons left: -10 Score; else -1 Score
  • HP-tiered scoring: <10% +10; <33% +8 (70%); <66% +7 (50%); else +7 (5%)

Final Gambit

  • Same last-mon penalty structure as Explosion
  • Faster, HP ≥ target’s: +8 Score; faster but can still finish the kill anyway: +7 Score; else (slower): +6 Score flat

Healing Wish, Lunar Dance

  • Blocked: 0 usable party mons, partner already doing the same thing, or the whole team (except the user) is already fully healed

Revival Blessing

  • Blocked if there’s no fainted party member at all
  • Discouraged (-10) if the AI can already win without it; discouraged (-20) if reviving would just die again before it matters (slower, target can KO)
  • Viability: +2 Score (50%) if a fainted mon genuinely exists

PRIORITY & COMBO MOVES

Fake Out

  • First Impression exempted from all of this (it’s the same effect ID but different move)
  • Blocked (-30) if it’s not literally the user’s first turn out
  • Blocked (-30) if target has Inner Focus/Shield Dust/Covert Cloak AND the move wouldn’t kill anyway
  • Viability: +9 Score flat, stacks with HDM

Feint

  • Doubles, target has a Protection move (not Wide/Quick Guard): +13 Score (~10.1%)
  • Else if it can outright kill the target: +6 Score (~10.1%)

Upper Hand

  • ~80% chance to just skip entirely (204/256 disqualifies)
  • Within the remaining ~20%: requires target to actually have a priority move, or -20
  • If it clears both: +13 Score (~10.1% — same probability gate reapplied in viability)

Pursuit

DOUBLES-SPECIFIC GENERAL RULES

The pipeline, precisely: for every potential target (both foes and the AI’s own partner), the AI runs the FULL scoring chain as if that were the only target, then picks whichever target’s best move scored highest. A move that CAN’T legally hit a given target (via CanTargetBattler) is skipped entirely for that target, not just scored low. Don’t use a move against your own ally if its score is under 100 — confirmed exact threshold, not an estimate: below 100 gets force-set to -1, disqualifying it.

Status Moves Targeting a Partner

Don’t get the default +6 just for being a status move. Only scored well (+6, 25% chance, immediate return — doesn’t stack with anything else) if the partner has: Volt Absorb/Earth Eater/Water Absorb/Dry Skin (HP≤75%), Lightning Rod (special moves, SpAtk can rise), Motor Drive (Speed can rise), Storm Drain (special moves, SpAtk can rise), Flash Fire (has a Fire move, not already boosted), Sap Sipper (physical moves, Attack can rise), or Contrary (move is a stat-lowering effect).

Weather Benefiting the Partner

Setting Sandstorm/Rain/Sun for a partner who’d genuinely benefit (via the same ShouldSet[Weather] checks used for the self-benefit case): +1 Score. Hail/Snow specifically: +2 Score (the partner must be alive to check Hail/Snow, a redundant-seeming but real distinct condition in the code).

Round

The game mechanic first: Round gets a power boost and acts immediately after the first user, regardless of normal Speed order, if a second Pokémon also uses Round the same turn. Two AI Pokémon chaining Round together is a deliberate, coded combo — not emergent behavior.

The exact code, quoted directly (two separate blocks in AI_DoubleBattle):

Block 1 — checking what the partner already chose (runs for every move, not just Round):

  • If partner’s already-chosen move is Round, AND this AI is SLOWER than that partner, AND this AI’s own move under consideration is also Round: +13 Score flat.

Block 2 — checking this AI’s own move choice, independent of what the partner already picked:

  • 90% chance of the whole block applying at all (10% it’s skipped outright).
  • Within that 90%: if this AI is FASTER than its partner, and the partner has Round in its moveset at all (whether or not it’s chosen anything yet): +7 Score to this AI’s own Round, AND the code force-writes +13 directly into the partner’s own stored Round score — bypassing the partner’s normal scoring process entirely for that one entry. The source’s own comment: “This ugly way is the only way to do it,” with a hardcoded assumption that Round always sits in moveset slot 0.

Engine order, per the source comment: Slot 1 always finishes its full move calculation first, with zero knowledge of what Slot 2 will do. Slot 2 calculates second, already knowing Slot 1’s pick.

  • Worked example — the combo working as intended:

  • Team is Torkoal in Slot 1 (slower, knows Round) and Dragapult in Slot 2 (faster, knows Round).

    1. Torkoal (Slot 1) evaluates first, with no knowledge of what Dragapult will choose. Round happens to already be a strong pick for Torkoal on its own merits (say, its best damage option) — it locks in Round using its own normal scoring, independent of anything Round-specific.
    1. Dragapult (Slot 2) evaluates second, now aware Torkoal already chose Round. Block 1 checks first: is Dragapult slower than Torkoal? No — Dragapult is faster, so this specific “join in because my partner already went” bonus does not apply to Dragapult.
    1. Block 2 then runs for Dragapult: is Dragapult faster than Torkoal (yes) and does Torkoal know Round (yes)? On the 90% roll: Dragapult’s own Round gets +7 Score, encouraging Dragapult to also pick it.
    1. Because the faster user (Dragapult) is the one sitting in Slot 2 here, the source’s own comment confirms this is the case where the override “sticks.” Dragapult picks Round.

  • Result: both Pokémon use Round. Since Dragapult is the second user to commit to it, it gets Round’s power boost and the turn-order jump per the actual game mechanic — the intended outcome.

  • Worked example — the same team, swapped positions, silently failing:

  • Same two Pokémon, but now Dragapult (faster) is in Slot 1 and Torkoal (slower) is in Slot 2.

    1. Dragapult (Slot 1) evaluates first, with no knowledge of what Torkoal will do yet — Torkoal hasn’t been evaluated at all.
    1. Block 2 runs for Dragapult: faster than Torkoal (yes), Torkoal knows Round (yes) — 90% roll gives Dragapult +7 Score on its own Round, and attempts to force-write +13 into Torkoal’s stored Round score.
    1. But Torkoal (Slot 2) hasn’t been evaluated yet at this point — it doesn’t calculate its own moves until after Dragapult’s entire turn-choice process finishes. When Torkoal’s own evaluation does run afterward, it computes its scores fresh, and that process doesn’t preserve or reference the earlier force-written +13 — Torkoal’s genuine calculation simply overwrites it.
    1. So whether Torkoal ends up picking Round on this turn now depends entirely on Round’s own ordinary merit for Torkoal (is it Torkoal’s best move anyway?) — the +13 nudge never actually influenced anything.

  • Result: Dragapult still picks Round on its own +7 bonus, but Torkoal may or may not follow — the combo isn’t reliably guaranteed the way it was in the first example, even though the code visibly tried to force it.

Honest limit on this explanation: the exact mechanism for why the override does or doesn’t “stick” — i.e., whether anything later in the broader battle engine re-reads that force-written score after the fact — depends on code outside this file (this file only shows the write happening and the source’s own comment describing the outcome). The two worked examples above follow the rule exactly as the source comments state it; I can’t independently trace the deeper wiring beyond what those comments assert without the rest of the engine.

  • Blocked unless: Doubles, partner alive, partner not duplicating, partner’s move isn’t status, partner isn’t switching out.
  • Follow Me viability: Player would kill the AI’s partner: +1 Score (50%)

Skill Swap — see Ability-Manipulation Moves section.

POST-KO SWITCH AI SCORES

(Confirmed to live in a separate file, not in this source — figures below are from the hand-written companion doc, cross-checked as consistent with everything else verified here.)

Calculated per available party member against the Player’s active Pokémon; highest score sent out, ties to party order. Doubles slots evaluated independently.

  • +5: AI’s mon faster and OHKOs

  • +4: AI’s mon slower and OHKOs (or Palafin-Zero)

  • +3: AI’s mon faster, KOs in ≤ some hit threshold (or Imposter Ditto)

  • +2: AI’s mon slower, KOs in < some hit threshold (or Wynaut/Wobbuffet)

  • +1: AI’s mon simply faster

  • 0: Default

  • -1: AI’s mon slower and would be OHKO’d (this specific check is skipped for Imposter Ditto candidates)

  • Support bonus: +2 Score, 10% of the time, if not already at -1. This is the ONLY place in Post-KO scoring where a Pokémon’s move-set-derived classification (rather than its raw stats/matchup) changes the number — every other line in this table is pure stat/type math, which makes this one exception worth noticing. It’s also deliberately small and low-probability (a 10%-of-the-time +2, not a flat guarantee) — consistent with it being a minor tiebreak-style nudge rather than the AI strongly preferring to send Support Pokémon out after a KO.

  • Switch-in chip damage: -1 if slower and 2HKO’d on entry; -2 if OHKO’d on entry.

MID-TURN SWITCH AI

(Same sourcing note as above.)

  • Default: only switches with a non-negative candidate, except Anti-Setup AI Plus.
  • Avoid Switch (blocks Walled/Support/Weather/Anti-Stall/Regenerator AI specifically) is active if: AI has boosted stats, AI can KO a Player mon, or Player has Pursuit/a phazing move/an OHKO move.

The Support AI trigger itself, in full: AI is classified Support, has been in for more than 1 turn, and Avoid Switch fails → 20% chance to switch out. Worth noting what this trigger does NOT require: it’s not conditioned on the Support Pokémon’s own HP, on whether it’s actually accomplished anything yet (set a hazard, applied a status), or on the board state beyond the shared Avoid Switch gate. It’s a flat, turn-count-gated chance — the AI doesn’t distinguish between a Support mon that just came in and one that’s already done its job. Note also this trigger has no Singles-only restriction the way Walled AI does — it’s confirmed to apply in Doubles too, based on the source’s own wording (only Walled AI is explicitly marked Singles-only).

Priority order: Perish Song (guaranteed) → Palafin (guaranteed if slower & about to be OHKO’d) → Anti-Setup AI (40%, random tiebreak) → Anti-Setup AI Plus (40%, random tiebreak, can go negative) → Walled AI (Singles only, 20%) → Support AI (20%) → Weather AI (20%) → Anti-Stall AI (20%) → Regenerator AI (40%) → Immunity AI (75%, bypasses Avoid Switch, matches Player’s last move type to an advantageous immunity ability).

  • Confirmed factored in: hazards, Tailwind, Trick Room, Wonder Room, Magic Room, Mud Sport, Water Sport, 0-PP moves (all except Sticky Web).
  • Not factored in: consumable items not yet on the field (Electric Seed, Booster Energy) — so Acrobatics/Unburden interactions aren’t predicted for switch-ins.

TURN-ORDER & MISC SCORING PASSES

Setup-First-Turn bonus

Turn 1 only, AI won’t die first: +2 Score for the full setup-move list (every stat move, Conversion, Light Screen, Focus Energy, Confuse, Substitute, Leech Seed, Minimize, Curse, Swagger/Flatter, Will-O-Wisp, Ingrain, Imprison, Tickle, Cosmic Power, Bulk Up, Calm Mind, Acupressure, Autotomize, Shift Gear, Shell Smash, Growth, Quiver Dance, Work Up, Hone Claws, all terrain/weather moves, Stealth Rock, Toxic Spikes, Trick Room, Wonder Room, Magic Room, Tailwind, Dragon Dance, Sticky Web, Geomancy, Victory Dance, generic hazards). AI would die first: -20 override instead.

Risky-move bonus

High-crit-ratio move: +2 Score flat. 50% coin flip, then +2 Score more for: Sleep, Explosion, Mirror Move, OHKO, Confuse, Metronome, Psywave, Counter, Destiny Bond, Swagger, Attract, Present, all-stats-up-on-hit, Belly Drum, Mirror Coat, Focus Punch, Revenge.

Prefer-Strongest-Move

Whichever move deals the most raw damage: +6 Score (75%)/+8 Score (25%).

  • Prefer-Baton-Pass (as its own scoring pass, layered on top of Baton Pass’s own viability entry above)
  • Turn 0, stat-raising: +5 Score. Later turns, HP≥60%: +1 Score. HP<60%: -10 Score. Ingrain/Aqua Ring not yet active: +2 Score. Baton Pass itself sums all current positive stages, +2 if rooted/aqua-ringed, -3 if Leech Seeded.

HP-Aware adjustments

  • Own HP high (>70%): -2 Score for Explosion, recovery moves, Rest, Destiny Bond, Flail, Endure, Memento, Grudge.
  • Own HP medium (30-70%): -2 for any stat move; -2 for Explosion, Bide, Conversion, Light Screen, Mist, Focus Energy, Conversion 2, Safeguard, Belly Drum.
  • Own HP low (<30%): -2 for any stat move; -2 for Bide, Conversion, Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil, Mist, Focus Energy, Rage, Conversion 2, Lock-On, Safeguard, Belly Drum, Psych Up, Mirror Coat, Solar Beam, Two-Turn attacks, Eruption, Tickle, weather moves.
  • Can outright KO this turn: +2 Score (stacks with the kill-bonus functions elsewhere — the kill signal gets reinforced across three separate functions, not just one).
  • Target HP medium, move is a stat-changer: -2 Score. Target HP low, move is status: -2 Score.
  • Targeting own partner with a healing-type effect while partner ≤50% HP: +1 Score.

SPECIAL-CONTEXT AI (Roaming, Safari, First Battle)

These replace all other scoring entirely when active — a Pokémon in one of these states doesn’t run the normal move-selection chain the same way.

Roaming Pokémon: if not currently trapped, ALWAYS flees — 100%, not chance-based. If trapped, behaves normally instead.

Safari Zone: flee chance is calculated as (an internal escape factor) × 5, giving a 0-20% range. Rolls below that flee; otherwise the Pokémon just watches (does nothing offensive that turn) rather than attacking.

First Battle (tutorial-style trainers): flees if the target’s HP is at or below 20%. This is a genuine flee trigger, not just move-avoidance.

  • AI_PreferBatonPass has a Protect-specific case not documented before: +2 Score normally, but -2 Score if the AI’s own last move was Protect or Detect (discourages passing right after already stalling a turn).

Corrections to AI_HPAware, now confirmed against the complete file:

  • When targeting your own partner specifically with Heal Pulse/Pollen Puff-style moves, OR any move whose type matches a partner ability that would heal them (Volt Absorb+Electric, Earth Eater+Ground, Water Absorb/Dry Skin+Water — note this specific list does NOT include Lightning Rod/Storm Drain/Sap Sipper/Motor Drive/Flash Fire/Contrary, which are handled by the separate Doubles-General ability-redirect check instead): Heal Block on the partner returns a hard 0; either opponent being able to KO the partner this turn: -1 Score (don’t bother healing a doomed ally); partner HP ≤50%: +1 Score.
